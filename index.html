Roastmaster's Craft

Precision in Every Roast.

Roasting is thermodynamic alchemy. In just twelve minutes, we guide green specialty beans through hundreds of chemical transformations — harnessing heat, airflow, and drum inertia to preserve origin character.

Giesen 15kg Cast Drum Real-time Cropster telemetry monitoring

The Roast Spectrum

Every green lot possesses a sweet spot where natural sugars, origin acids, and aromatic volatiles reach equilibrium.

Light Roast (City) Drop: ~202°C

Accentuates natural citric/malic acidity, floral aromatics, and terroir clarity. Minimal roast taint.

Medium Roast (City+) Drop: ~212°C

Sweet spot of the Maillard reaction. Balanced acidity, pronounced caramel sweetness, and round body.

Dark Roast (Vienna) Drop: ~224°C

Gentle entry into second crack. Caramelized wood sugars, bittersweet molten cacao, and syrupy crema.

Step-By-Step Journey

From Green Bean to Cupping Bowl

STAGE 01

Moisture Evaporation

Beans enter drum at 200°C. Initial charge temperature drops. Moisture evaporates from 11% to bone dryness as beans turn pale yellow.

STAGE 02

The Maillard Phase

Amino acids and natural sugars interact vigorously at 160°C. Baked bread aromas emerge as beans turn light cinnamon brown and swell.

STAGE 03

First Crack Event

Internal steam pressure fractures the cellular bean walls with an audible pop around 196°C. Bean volume doubles; true coffee aroma is born.

STAGE 04

Rapid Air Cooling

Discharged onto stainless cooling trays with high-volume vacuum suction. Temperature halted within 90 seconds to lock in volatile aromatics.

EMBER & ORIGIN was born out of an uncompromising obsession with single-origin coffee transparency. Founded in an industrial brick warehouse by a pair of Q-graders, we set out to strip away pretension and celebrate the real agricultural wonders of coffee origin.

We roast exclusively in micro-batches of under fifteen kilograms. This allows us to calibrate gas burner curves, airflow velocity, and drum speeds with surgical precision for every distinct harvest lot.
